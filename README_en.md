## 📖 Overview

Design spent 20 years ascending from "aesthetics" to the C-suite agenda. McKinsey proved that companies with high design maturity outperformed industry peers by 32 points in revenue growth. The UK design economy generated £97.4 billion in GVA.

Yet the very design firms that drove this ascent paradoxically declined. IDEO collapsed from $300M to $100M in revenue. Over 70 design firms were acquired.

Now, generative AI is forcing a second, more fundamental redefinition of design.

This book decodes the structure of this upheaval through a single lens:

**Design and business are not separate disciplines. They are different expressions of the same cognitive process — seeing the essence and structuring it. Now that generative AI has claimed execution, this shared foundation — the "eye" — is the only weapon that remains in the AI era.**

## 🏗️ Structure

This book consists of 6 chapters.

| Chapter | Title | Content |
|---------|-------|---------|
| Chapter 1 | The Same Eye, a Different World | The discovery reached through cross-disciplinary experience as a BTC talent — design and business are different expressions of the same cognitive process. The "lens" that runs through the entire book |
| Chapter 2 | When Design Changed Business | IDEO, Tim Brown, Stanford d.school. The 20-year history of how the "eye" was brought into business and elevated to a management agenda |
| Chapter 3 | The Paradox: Design's Value Rose While Design Firms Died | IDEO's collapse, 70+ acquisitions. The structural separation of the "eye" from "method packages." The common structure of survivors |
| Chapter 4 | What AI Took, What It Couldn't | The collapse of execution's value and the inviolability of the "eye." The structure predicted by Cross, Schön, and Dorst's design theories |
| Chapter 5 | The Collapse of Binary Oppositions — Where Design and Business Become One | "Creative vs Logical," "Right brain vs Left brain" — false boundaries dissolve, converging at the summit |
| Chapter 6 | The Design Strategist in the AI Era | The 4-step practice of the "eye." Connection to Depth & Velocity. The role of technology and the scarcity of BTC talent |
